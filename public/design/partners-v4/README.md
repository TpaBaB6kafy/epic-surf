Each photograph has a 480px-wide `-preview.webp` companion. Sharp performs encoding/resize only. Texture quality 82; photographs 88; alpha artwork 94 with alpha quality 100.

Surfer and hands are generated adaptations of the user-supplied illustrations. Hands omit the original poster copy and background. The surfer's scarlet fork-tail fish is based on the supplied EPIC board photograph. The painted pose and white spray follow the original illustration; underneath the board is transparency rather than blue water. The three generated photographs illustrate collaboration formats and are not documentary pictures of existing partners.

Original PNGs, prompts, earlier surfer exports and screenshots remain in local output folders, outside this release. CSS chooses focal points for each photograph and breakpoint. The hero and final CTA share a non-repeating texture sampled at the same scale. Desktop hands are proportionally capped at 460px; phones use their separately approved rotated composition. Hero background path bands have been removed.
