# Partner page v4 — 2026-10-08

The approved partner page now uses six sections in the EPIC palette: a painted surfer hero, audience list, partnership-format photo slider, four-step process, benefits/referral attribution and a matching hands CTA. Ordinal labels and hero path bands are removed. The hero uses the branded scarlet fish and white spray; desktop hands are proportional and capped at 460px. EN/RU routes and commercial terms are preserved.

The primary CTA opens an email request form with validation, pending, error, rate-limit and confirmed-success states. Discussion CTAs use Telegram for RU and WhatsApp for EN; the VI Zalo mapping is prepared without creating a Vietnamese route. The existing lesson footer is extracted as SiteFooter and reused; the partner page reveals its original contacts/map across the full viewport. Header booking, messenger links, tracking, language queries and partner attribution are retained. No email enters analytics.

The user explicitly approved commit, push and deployment. Release scope excludes the independent homepage sunset edits and local output/tmp material. Only active partner assets are published.

Validation before publication: production build and scoped ESLint; 125 existing behavior checks; 53 email form, 28 isolated API, 4 contact/analytics and 2 landscape-access checks. Responsive screenshots cover EN/RU from 320 to 3200px. Canonical, alternates and structured data are unchanged; no horizontal overflow, missing page imagery or browser exceptions were found. External booking/map/messenger services were stubbed and no actual requests/messages were sent. The form regression script now mocks every submission for safe reruns; production smoke verification opens the form without submitting it.
